About the System

Smart Clinic Analytics and Decision Support System

This system helps the Alangilan Senior High School clinic track visit patterns, common illnesses, and supply levels for Grade 11 and Grade 12 students across STEM, ABM, and HUMSS strands. It flags unusual increases in specific illness types and suggests relevant health campaigns. No student names are collected, only grade level, strand, and illness category, to keep the system privacy-safe while still useful for planning.
