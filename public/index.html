About the System

Smart Clinic Analytics and Decision Support System

This system helps the Alangilan Senior High School clinic track visit patterns, common illnesses, and supply levels for Grade 11 and Grade 12 students. It flags unusual increases in consultation illness types and suggests relevant health campaigns. Student names are recorded only for Feeding entries; Clinic Consultation check-ins do not collect student names. The dashboard uses purpose, grade, section, and health details for clinic planning.
